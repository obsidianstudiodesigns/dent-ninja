STEP 1 - REPOINT THE MX RECORD (please action on its own, first)
------------------------------------------------------------------

The MX record currently points at the bare domain:

    dentninja.co.za.   MX   0   dentninja.co.za.
    dentninja.co.za.   A        196.41.122.211

Because mail follows that MX to the domain's own A record, changing the A
record in step 2 would route mail for the domain to GitHub, which runs no mail
server, and all incoming email would bounce.

Please change the MX to the dedicated mail hostname:

    Type:     MX
    Name:     dentninja.co.za
    Value:    mail.dentninja.co.za
    Priority: 0

mail.dentninja.co.za already exists and already resolves to 196.41.122.211, so
no new record is needed for it. This is also the hostname given as our POP3 and
SMTP server in your welcome email, so mail delivery is unaffected by the change.

The current TTL is 14400 (4 hours). Please allow that to expire and confirm mail
is still being delivered before moving on to step 2. If it is easier, you are
welcome to drop the TTL to 300 beforehand so the change can be completed in one
session.
