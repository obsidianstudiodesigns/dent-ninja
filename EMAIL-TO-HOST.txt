------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 - MAKE MAIL INDEPENDENT (please action on its own, first)
------------------------------------------------------------------

The zone currently resolves mail through the domain's own A record, twice over:

    dentninja.co.za.        A       196.41.122.211
    mail.dentninja.co.za.   CNAME   dentninja.co.za
    dentninja.co.za.        MX  0   dentninja.co.za

Both the MX target and the mail hostname are CNAME/self-referential back to the
apex. Changing the apex A record in step 2 would therefore route mail for the
domain to GitHub, which runs no mail server, and all incoming email would
bounce. Repointing MX alone would not help, because mail.dentninja.co.za is a
CNAME to the apex and would follow it.

Could you please make mail.dentninja.co.za an independent A record, and then
point the MX at it:

    1. Delete the CNAME:   mail.dentninja.co.za  ->  dentninja.co.za

    2. Add an A record:
           Name:    mail.dentninja.co.za
           Type:    A
           Value:   196.41.122.211
           TTL:     300

    3. Update the MX record:
           Name:     dentninja.co.za
           Type:     MX
           Value:    mail.dentninja.co.za
           Priority: 0
           TTL:      300

mail.dentninja.co.za is the POP3/SMTP hostname given in our welcome email, so
this also brings the zone in line with your own documented mail settings. As a
secondary benefit it resolves a standards issue, since an MX record should not
resolve to a CNAME (RFC 2181).

The current TTL is 14400 (4 hours). Please allow that to expire and confirm mail
is still being delivered before moving on to step 2. If it is easier, you are
welcome to drop the TTL to 300 beforehand so the change can be completed in one
session.
